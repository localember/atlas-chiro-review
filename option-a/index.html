Working adults

Student athletes

Pregnancy and kids

Dr. X is Webster certified and loves prenatal work. She also sees babies and kids, with techniques scaled to their size. More on that below.

Cold laser therapy, for pain that keeps coming back.

We use the Erchonia FX 635, an FDA-cleared low-level laser. It targets pain at the source and helps calm inflammation, without drugs and without downtime.

It's especially useful for chronic low back pain and injuries that are slow to heal. You can add it to an adjustment or massage, or come in just for laser.

Pregnant, postpartum, or bringing in a little one?

Prenatal care is some of Dr. X's favorite work. She's Webster certified, she adjusts gently, and she modifies treatment as your pregnancy progresses so you're comfortable at every stage. After the baby comes, we help with the back and pelvic pain that tends to linger.

Kids get the same attention, from infants through teenagers. Playground tumbles, sports and heavy backpacks all add up, and the techniques are always adapted to your child's age and size. If you're not sure chiropractic is right for your child, call and ask. We're happy to talk it through.

Meet Dr. X.

Dr. Xidos found chiropractic through her own back pain, and opened Atlas in June 2005. Plenty of her patients have trusted her with more than one generation of their family.

She's a manual, full-body chiropractor. That means hands-on adjustments for your whole body, not the Atlas Orthogonal technique, despite the name on the door.

Patients tend to say the same things about her. She's thorough, she explains what's going on, and she doesn't rush. One patient still drives up from Lakewood.

Aimee Hernandez, our licensed massage therapist, has worked alongside her since 2016. New massage patients go on a wait list, so call or text to get on it.

You'll probably meet Bear too. He's Dr. X's German Shepherd and the office's self-appointed quality control supervisor. Patients who want a little dog time get it.
