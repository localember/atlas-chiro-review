Meet the Team

Atlas Chiropractic Health Center was founded by Dr. A. Corinne Xidos, who brought her commitment to whole-person wellness and compassionate care to Seattle’s Capitol Hill and First Hill neighborhoods in June 2005.

Book a new patient exam Text (206) 324-2225

After discovering chiropractic through her own experience with back pain, Dr. Xidos dedicated her career to helping others lead healthier and more comfortable lives. What began as a small, patient-centered practice has grown into a close-knit team offering chiropractic care, massage therapy, and holistic health services in a supportive and welcoming environment.

Located in the heart of First Hill, Atlas Chiropractic Health Center proudly serves Seattle and surrounding communities. Our practice has built strong relationships with patients and their families, many of whom trust us with their care across multiple generations. Whether you are new to chiropractic or seeking a more personalized approach, you will find a friendly, community-oriented atmosphere where we take the time to understand your health goals and treat you with genuine care.

We welcome patients of all ages and backgrounds, including athletes, active individuals, expectant mothers, children, and those recovering from injuries or looking to improve their long-term wellness. Our services include advanced chiropractic care, massage therapy, laser therapy, corrective exercises, and individualized wellness guidance. Each treatment plan is tailored to support your overall well-being, encourage active participation, and empower you to take charge of your health.

We invite you to meet our providers and learn more about the team that makes Atlas Chiropractic Health Center a trusted partner in your wellness journey. Meet them below.

Staff

Chrissy

Office Manager

Originally from Panama City, Florida, Chrissy moved to Seattle in 2021 after several years living in Savannah and Atlanta. She brings more than a decade of customer service and administrative experience to the clinic and oversees insurance billing, authorizations, appeals, benefit verification, and patient concerns. Before entering the healthcare field, Chrissy spent ten years working as a special needs nanny, giving her extensive experience navigating diverse personalities, needs, and communication styles. Outside the office, she enjoys reading, attending concerts, and watching Chinese and Korean dramas, and she is always happy to share recommendations.

Pronounced: kriss-ee.

Falisha

Front Desk Admin

Born and raised in Seattle, Falisha has a deep appreciation for the city and the community she grew up in. She brings a strong background in care-focused work, including nannying, caregiving, and doula support, before transitioning into healthcare administration. At Atlas Chiropractic Health Center, she assists with patient relations, scheduling, insurance verification, and front desk communication. Known for her warm and upbeat personality, she helps create a welcoming environment for every patient who walks through the door. Outside the office, she enjoys spending time with her family, creating and admiring art, playing cozy games, and watching films with friends.

Pronounced: fuh-LEE-sha.

Bear

Quality Control Supervisor

Bear is Atlas Chiropractic Health Center’s beloved German Shepherd and unofficial Quality Control Supervisor. He takes his role very seriously and can often be found greeting patients, supervising office activities, or napping in strategic locations. Bear brings calm, comfort, and a sense of home to the clinic, making visits more enjoyable for patients of all ages. Whether he is offering a quiet presence during treatments or simply keeping watch over his humans, Bear is an essential part of the ACHC team and a favorite among regular visitors.

Pronounced “bear,” like the animal.
