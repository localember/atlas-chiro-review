Cold laser therapy in Seattle

A non-invasive option for pain management and healing, without medication, injections or surgery. Add it to an adjustment, or come in just for laser.

At Atlas Chiropractic Health Center, we are excited to offer laser therapy as a cutting-edge, non-invasive option for pain management and healing.

Our Seattle, WA office uses state-of-the-art devices like the Erchonia FX 635, which is FDA-cleared as an adjunct to provide relief of minor chronic low back pain of musculoskeletal origin, and backed by clinical research for effectiveness and safety. Laser therapy works by delivering specific wavelengths of light to targeted tissues, reducing inflammation and encouraging healing at a cellular level. This can help get you back to your favorite Seattle activities, all without the need for medication, injections, or surgery.

We know that chronic pain can hold you back from living your best life. Our laser therapy services are designed to give you another option for relief and long-term wellness, whether you’re managing a work-related injury, recovering from a sports mishap, or looking to support your regular chiropractic care. Our team will explain everything up front and answer all your questions, so you can make the best decision for your care.

Conditions laser therapy can help treat

Many people experience chronic back pain, muscle aches, or joint discomfort that just won’t go away with rest or other treatments. We see Seattle patients struggling with ongoing lower back pain, stiffness from old injuries, sports trauma, or even pain after spending long hours working in front of a computer. Laser therapy is a great option for those who want targeted, gentle relief, particularly when other methods haven’t provided the comfort they need.

  • Chronic low back pain. The Erchonia FX 635 is FDA-cleared as an adjunct to provide relief of minor chronic low back pain of musculoskeletal origin.
  • Stiffness from old injuries and sports trauma
  • Desk-day pain after long hours at the computer
  • Pain that limits hobbies, like hiking, biking or playing with your kids or grandkids

Some patients are looking for an alternative to pain medications or want a recovery boost after an injury. Whether you have chronic or sudden pain, laser therapy can be a helpful piece of your overall wellness plan.

The laser therapy process

Our team explains exactly how it works and answers your questions before anything starts.

  1. We talk firstWe’ll discuss your pain history, previous treatments, and your goals for relief.
  2. Your sessionYou’re positioned comfortably while the laser targets the painful area. You might feel a gentle warmth or tingling, but it’s typically painless, with no burning or discomfort.
  3. Back to your dayTreatments are quick, and you can return to your regular activities right after your appointment.
  4. A series, adjusted as you goMany people notice some improvement after just a few sessions, but a series of visits is often recommended for lasting results. We monitor your progress and adjust your plan as needed.

How laser therapy can help you

People who receive laser therapy may experience reduced pain, inflammation, and improved range of motion. Our Seattle patients often tell us that after a few sessions, they can move more freely, return to work or exercise sooner, and rely less on pain medication. Many also find that laser therapy supports a faster recovery, especially when dealing with long-term pain or stubborn injuries. Because it’s non-invasive and doesn’t involve medication, laser therapy offers a safe, comfortable way to manage discomfort while supporting the body’s natural healing mechanisms.

While not everyone gets the same results, we frequently see patients report less day-to-day pain, improved sleep, and a better overall mood after completing a series of laser therapy sessions. Our goal is always to help you move better, feel better, and get back to doing what you love.
