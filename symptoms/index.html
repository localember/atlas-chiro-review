Neck pain

Neck pain is a widespread issue that can develop gradually due to poor posture, repetitive activities, or suddenly after injury. Symptoms may include stiffness, sharp or aching sensations, and reduced mobility, and they can often radiate into the shoulders, arms, or upper back. Prolonged desk work, smartphone use, and stress are frequent contributors, while more severe cases may arise from whiplash, arthritis, or spinal misalignment.

Back pain

Back pain is one of the most common complaints, affecting a significant portion of adults at some point in their lives. It can present as sharp, aching, or throbbing discomfort anywhere from the lower to the upper back, often interfering with activities such as sitting, standing, walking, or even sleeping. Common causes include muscle strain, ligament injuries, poor posture, herniated discs, arthritis, and repetitive motion or workplace stress. Chronic or severe back pain can impact one’s physical, emotional, and social well-being, keeping people from their favorite hobbies or essential daily tasks.

Learn more

Headaches & migraines

Headaches and migraines are common symptoms that can range from mild, occasional discomfort to debilitating attacks impacting work, recreation, and social connections. Tension-type headaches may cause a dull, pressing pain around the temples or forehead, while migraines often present with throbbing on one side, sensitivity to light and sound, and can be accompanied by visual disturbances or nausea. Common triggers include stress, muscular tension (often from poor posture), certain foods, hormonal changes, environmental factors, and spinal misalignment.

Learn more

Shoulder pain

Shoulder pain can arise from injuries, overuse, poor posture, or underlying conditions such as rotator cuff strains, bursitis, tendinitis, or arthritis. It often presents as aching, stiffness, decreased range of motion, or sharp pain with certain movements. People may notice shoulder pain when reaching, lifting, or even during sleep, leading to challenges with daily functioning and recreational activities.

Learn more

Arm & leg pain

Arm and leg pain encompasses a wide spectrum of symptoms, from aching muscles and joint discomfort to sharp, shooting nerve pain, numbness, or tingling. Common causes range from repetitive strain, injuries, and sports overuse to nerve compression (such as carpal tunnel syndrome or sciatica) and underlying spinal issues. Such pain can impact typing, lifting, walking, or even enjoying your favorite outdoor activities.

Learn more

Scoliosis

Scoliosis is characterized by an abnormal lateral curvature of the spine, which can develop during childhood or adolescence but also occurs in adults. Individuals may experience uneven shoulders, waist, or hips, and in more pronounced cases, visible spinal curves or trunk imbalance. Scoliosis can lead to pain, reduced mobility, muscle fatigue, and even breathing issues, especially as the condition progresses. Many people feel self-conscious or frustrated by the changes scoliosis brings to their posture and daily life.
