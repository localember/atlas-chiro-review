Gentle care for pregnancy, new moms and kids.

Prenatal care is some of Dr. X's favorite work. She uses gentle techniques and modifies treatment so you're comfortable at every stage, and after delivery she helps with the aches that hang around. Prenatal massage may be available too, depending on your stage.

For kids, she adjusts patients from newborns to teens. Growth spurts, sports, playground falls and heavy backpacks all put stress on a growing body. If you're wondering whether chiropractic makes sense for your child, give us a call. We're happy to talk about it.

Cold laser therapy for stubborn pain.

Our Erchonia FX 635 is an FDA-cleared low-level laser. It targets pain at its source and helps reduce inflammation, and many people find it a welcome option when they'd rather avoid medication. It works on its own or alongside an adjustment or massage.
