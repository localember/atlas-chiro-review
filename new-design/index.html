Pregnancy and pediatric care is a specialty here.

It's not the whole practice, but it's work Dr. X loves, and she's trained for it.

During and after pregnancy

Dr. X is Webster certified. She uses gentle techniques and modifies treatment at every stage, then helps with lingering back and pelvic pain after delivery. Prenatal massage may be an option too, depending on your stage.

Babies and kids

She sees patients from infancy through the teen years, with techniques adapted to their age, size and comfort. Not sure if it's right for your child? Call and ask. We're happy to talk it through.
