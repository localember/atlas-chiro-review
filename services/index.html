Chiropractic services in Seattle

Welcome to the comprehensive range of services provided by Atlas Chiropractic Health Center, where your health and well-being come first.

Book a new patient exam Text (206) 324-2225

Through expert chiropractic adjustments and pain management, we help relieve discomfort, restore mobility, and guide you toward a healthier, more vibrant life. Our practice delivers personalized, natural solutions, serving patients at every stage of life, from newborns to seniors. Let us help you move better, feel better, and live better every day.

Chiropractic care

Chiropractic care focuses on correcting misalignments to improve how your spine and the rest of your body function. Drug-free and non-invasive.

Learn more

Cold laser therapy

A non-invasive, drug-free way to address chronic pain for people looking for alternatives to medication or surgery. We use the Erchonia FX 635.

Learn more

Pregnancy & postnatal

Gentle, supportive adjustments to help mothers manage the physical changes and stresses of pregnancy, and to return to comfort after delivery.

Learn more

Pediatric chiropractic

Safe, gentle adjustments to support your child’s growth and development, making visits a positive, comfortable experience from infancy through the teenage years.

Learn more

Corrective exercise

Corrective exercises are focused movement strategies that help restore healthy function and address limitations in your joints and muscles.

Learn more

Massage therapy

A hands-on approach that helps relieve muscle tension, promote relaxation, and support healing for a variety of aches and pains.
