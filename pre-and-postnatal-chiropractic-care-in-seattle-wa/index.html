Prenatal and postnatal chiropractic in Seattle

At Atlas Chiropractic Health Center, we know that pregnancy and postpartum recovery bring both joyful moments and real physical challenges for moms.

Book a new patient exam Text (206) 324-2225

Our pre and postnatal chiropractic care is designed to provide gentle, non-invasive support for your entire journey, including before, during, and after childbirth. From your first trimester aches to the postpartum healing process, we use the safest, most nurturing techniques to keep you comfortable and confident through every stage. Our skilled team creates a welcoming space where you can relax, share your concerns, and know you’re in caring hands right here in Seattle.

We take the time to get to know your unique needs and tailor each adjustment to your changing body. As part of the greater Seattle community, we’re here to help growing families find natural relief from back pain, pelvic discomfort, and related pregnancy challenges, always prioritizing the safety and wellness of both mother and child.

Signs You Might Need Pre and Postnatal Chiropractic

Many expectant mothers experience low back pain, hip discomfort, sciatica, or trouble finding a comfortable sleep position as pregnancy progresses. We also hear about pelvic pain, stiffness in the hips and lower back, or issues with balance and posture. Some moms-to-be visit us for relief from headaches, neck tension, or stress, which can be magnified during this special time. After delivery, patients often notice lingering back or pelvic pain, tightness from childbirth, or new aches related to holding or nursing their baby.

If you’re feeling "out of balance," sore, or notice that everyday movements are harder during or after pregnancy, pre and postnatal chiropractic care in Seattle may provide the comfort and support you’re looking for. We welcome you whether you’re new to chiropractic or have loved it for years.

What to Expect During Pre and Postnatal Chiropractic

When you visit us for pre or postnatal chiropractic care in Seattle, we’ll start with a gentle assessment and an open conversation about your symptoms and concerns. We want you to feel completely comfortable, so we always explain each step and modify your care for your trimester or postpartum recovery stage. Our adjustments are gentle and carefully chosen to protect you and your baby; we use special tables and pillows to safely position you, and techniques that ease discomfort without any unnecessary force.

We'll check in with you about changes since your last visit and answer questions about posture, sleeping, and staying active. After childbirth, we continue to support you as your body adapts, helping you rebuild strength and return to your daily Seattle routines with confidence.

Benefits of Pre and Postnatal Chiropractic

Many moms who receive pre and postnatal chiropractic care at our Seattle practice share that they experience less back or pelvic pain, easier movement, and a greater sense of relaxation throughout and after pregnancy. Our gentle adjustments may help improve sleep and boost energy, making it easier to handle the demands of pregnancy, birth, and new motherhood. Some patients report shorter, more comfortable labor, or less postpartum discomfort, although results vary from person to person.

Most importantly, many women tell us they feel genuinely supported, not just physically, but also emotionally, by our caring Seattle team. Our goal is to help you thrive during and after this incredible life transition, so you can focus on growing and nurturing your new family with comfort and confidence.
